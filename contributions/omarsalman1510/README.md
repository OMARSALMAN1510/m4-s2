This chart shows total revenue by city based on data from the Amman Digital Market integration project. Revenue was calculated by multiplying the quantity of items in each order by the product unit price, then grouping the results by customer city.

The chart highlights that Amman generates the highest revenue compared to other cities. This indicates that most of the market activity is concentrated in the capital.

To ensure accuracy, cancelled orders and records with missing city values were excluded from the analysis. This helps provide a clearer view of actual completed transactions.

This insight is useful for understanding where business performance is strongest and can support decisions related to expansion and targeting.
